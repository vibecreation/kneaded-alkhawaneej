OUR STORY

Baked slow, kneaded by hand

Kneaded started as a home kitchen in Al Khawaneej with one sourdough starter and a lot of patience. Every loaf is still hand-shaped and slow-fermented, every pastry laminated the old way. No shortcuts, no rush — just proper bread and butter-rich viennoiserie made the way it should be.
